How to Delete Your Data In-App (Recommended)

You can delete your data immediately inside the Family Clock application:

  1. Open Family Clock on your Android device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll down to PRIVACY & DATA.
  4. Tap Delete account & data.
  5. Confirm deletion on the pop-up warning dialog.

Warning: In-app deletion permanently removes your local personal alarms, app preferences, user document (users/{uid}), removes your membership from shared family alarms, and deletes your Firebase user session.

How to Request Manual Web Deletion

If you no longer have access to the Android application, you may request manual data deletion by contacting our privacy team:

Data Retention & Processing After Deletion Request

  • Deleted Immediately: Local device database, local settings, user document (users/{uid}), and active pairing codes.
  • Preserved for Other Members: Shared family alarms created by other household members will remain active for remaining members, but your user ID is removed from membership.
  • Third-Party Financial Logs: Subscriptions processed via Google Play Billing may retain anonymized transaction records in Google Play Console for legal, tax, and audit compliance required by Google Play Policies.
